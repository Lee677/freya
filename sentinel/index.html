03

Rules of engagement

The interlocks aren't a feature list — they're the reason this is allowed near a boat.

HOLD FIRE

Above the horizon

If the target is in the sky, the turret tracks but the laser stays dark. The beam physically cannot rise to horizontal — sky and aircraft are off‑limits by design.

CLEARED

On the boat

Below the horizon line — i.e. actually landing on the deck — the effector pulses and sweeps. That's also precisely when the mess happens.

PROTECTED

Native species

Friendlies in the bush sit outside the frame and the envelope. The system deters nuisance visitors to the vessel, nothing else.

FAIL‑SAFE

On any fault

Lost frame, no target, or power blip → laser off, servos park centre. Default state is dark and still.
