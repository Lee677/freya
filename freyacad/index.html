freyacad · wrong-sized workshop

This bench doesn’t fold down to phone size.

freyacad is a full parametric CAD bench — a real solid-modelling kernel, sketches, fillets, booleans — and it steers with a mouse, a keyboard and a monitor’s worth of elbow room. On a touchscreen it would be all thumbs. Literally.

Open it on a PC or a Mac and the whole workshop will be set up and waiting for you.
